Privacy Policy — Lancer Character Tracker

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Lancer Character Tracker ("the App") is a free, unofficial fan-made character tracker for the Lancer tabletop role-playing game. It is a static web page hosted on GitHub Pages. The App has no server of its own and no database.

1. Data stored on your device

Your characters, mech configurations, and app settings are stored in your browser's local storage (localStorage) on your own device. This data never leaves your device unless you use the optional Google Drive sync described below.

2. Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, the App uses Google Identity Services and the Google Drive API with the following scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — access only to files created by the App or opened by you with the App.

The App does not request full access to your Google Drive. It cannot see, read, or modify any other files in your Drive. Files the App creates are stored in your own Google Drive account.

You can revoke the App's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops future syncing, but does not delete data already stored in your Drive or in your browser; you can delete those yourself.

3. Google sign-in

When you sign in with Google, Google may share your name, email address, and profile picture with the App as part of the standard sign-in process. The App uses this information only to identify your account for sync. It does not share it with anyone else.

4. What we do not collect

The App contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. The page loads Google's sign-in library and communicates with the Google Drive API; it does not send your data to any other third party.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. The only data transfers are to Google's services (your own Google account) when you sign in or use Drive sync.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains in your browser and/or your Google Drive until you delete it: clear your browser's site data to remove local data, delete the App's files in Google Drive, and/or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page, and the effective date above will be updated.

9. Contact
